About UMA SEO AI

UMA SEO AI is an automated, free SEO agent that runs automatically every day on GitHub Actions. It helps manage and optimize website performance through on-page SEO audits, content generation, competitor comparisons, and automated site monitoring.

On-Page SEO Auditing and Speed Measurement

The agent conducts daily automated audits focused on essential on-page SEO factors to evaluate your website. The audit covers the following key components:

  • Title tags and meta description tags
  • Page headings and structural elements
  • Optimization of images and internal or external links
  • Structured data and schema
  • Technical files, specifically robots.txt and the site sitemap

In addition to on-page audits, UMA SEO AI measures website speed directly with Google PageSpeed Insights to track performance metrics.

AI Content Generation and Keyword Discovery

UMA SEO AI identifies new keyword ideas gathered directly from Google Autocomplete. It then utilizes Gemini AI to write articles, generate tags, and build a customized action plan tailored to your site.

On-Page Competitor Comparisons

Understanding the competitive landscape is simplified through on-page SEO analysis. UMA SEO AI compares the target site directly with competitor websites on on-page SEO details. It does not show competitor search volume or backlinks because that data is paid.

Automated Hourly Monitoring and Site Rollbacks

Website stability is continuously maintained through automated safety checks. A dedicated monitor checks the site every hour to ensure everything functions properly. If the site breaks, the monitor automatically rolls the site back to the last working version.
